## O PROMPT (copiar daqui para baixo)

```
# Logo exploration brief — Skillset Mind

## Who we are

Skillset Mind (skillsetmind.com) is a course platform — marketplace plus
members area — built specifically for professionals who work with the human
mind: psychologists, therapists, coaches and mentors. They come to learn
verifiable skills, teach their own methods, and earn credentials that
actually mean something.

Positioning in one line: **"Real competence for those who work with the
mind — proven, not promised."** Working tagline: *"Where the mind's work
becomes proven skill."*

Brand attributes, in priority order:
1. Trustworthy / credible — this audience is trained to detect hype
2. Serious / rigorous — evidence and verification over promises
3. Human / warm — we serve people who care for people; not a cold SaaS
4. Verifiable / proven — credentials, certificates, real outcomes
5. Premium / differentiated — above the commodity course-platform tier

What we must NOT look like (anti-attributes):
- A guru / internet-marketing course brand (hype, arrows going up, money energy)
- A meditation or wellness app (lotus, zen circles, pastel calm clichés)
- A commodity course marketplace (interchangeable with Udemy/Hotmart)
- A cold enterprise SaaS (soulless geometry with no warmth)
- Amateur / DIY (clip-art brains, gradients doing the heavy lifting)

## How to read the attached reference images

I attached ~31 images in three groups. Study them before designing:

1. **Direct competitors** (Kiwify, Thinkific, Teachable, Kajabi, Hotmart and
   similar course platforms). Extract the CATEGORY CONVENTION: one-letter
   monogram or simple abstract mark + one strong color + lowercase geometric
   sans wordmark. This is what "course platform" looks like today. We want to
   be recognizable as a modern platform — but if our logo could be swapped
   with any of theirs, we failed. Learn the convention so you can break it
   deliberately.

2. **Premium non-competitor marks** (navy + gold, seals, credential and
   heritage cues). Extract the TRUST SIGNALS: how institutions communicate
   "this credential is real" — emblematic weight, restrained palettes,
   confident type. We want that credibility WITHOUT looking like a
   200-year-old university or a law firm.

3. **Work by Rob Janoff** (the designer of the Apple logo). Extract the
   METHOD, not the style: a simple letterform or object carrying a hidden
   second meaning; geometry so reduced it survives at 16px; a mark that works
   in one color, in positive and negative space; rounded-yet-strong forms
   that feel friendly without losing authority. Every concept you propose
   should pass the Janoff test: "can you describe the idea in one sentence,
   and does it have a second read that rewards a closer look?"

## The creative challenge

Fuse the three: the MODERN SIMPLICITY of the platform category, the
CREDENTIAL TRUST of the premium marks, and the LAYERED MEANING of Janoff's
method — applied to our territory: the mind, mastery, and proof.

Semantic territory to mine (free association welcome): mind / thought /
insight · dialogue and the therapeutic relationship · growth and progression
· skill and craft · verification, seal, proof · the "S" and "M" letterforms
· depth (what's beneath the surface) · the moment of clarity.

Cliché blacklist — do not use: brain outlines (with or without circuits),
lightbulbs, gears-in-head, lotus flowers, meditating figures, chat bubbles,
graduation caps, upward arrows/charts, handshakes, puzzle pieces.

## Deliverables

Propose **5 DISTINCT concept directions** — genuinely different ideas, not
five variations of one idea. Suggested spread (adapt if you have better
instincts):

- A) A monogram (S, M, or an S+M ligature) hiding a second meaning
- B) An abstract symbol for mind/insight/depth — no letterforms
- C) A modernized credential mark — seal/emblem energy, radically simplified
- D) A wordmark-first identity where one typographic detail carries the idea
- E) A negative-space concept (the hidden read IS the negative space)

For EACH direction, show:
1. The mark alone (large)
2. Horizontal lockup: mark + "Skillset Mind" wordmark
3. The mark at small size (favicon/app-icon simulation)
4. One-color version on light AND on dark background
5. A 2–3 sentence rationale: the idea, the hidden second meaning, and why it
   fits a platform trusted by therapists and psychologists

## Hard constraints

- Must survive at 16px favicon size — if it needs detail to work, simplify it
- Must work in ONE flat color (no gradient-dependent concepts)
- Must work on light and dark surfaces
- Wordmark: "Skillset Mind" (two words, or "skillsetmind" if lowercase
  single-word treatment is stronger — show your pick and say why)
- Color: our product already lives on a deep navy foundation; navy is a safe
  home base and gold/brass is a natural premium accent — but you have freedom
  to propose a different palette per concept if the idea demands it. One
  strong brand color per concept, not rainbow systems.

Take risks across the five directions — I'd rather reject two bold ideas
than choose between five safe ones. Present all five side by side so I can
compare and select.
```

## Decisão em aberto (afeta a direção C — selo/credencial)

O posicionamento assume "credencial que vale algo". Falta decidir: os
certificados serão **apenas verificados pela plataforma** ou buscaremos
**credenciamento oficial (CE credits / conselhos profissionais)**? Se for o
segundo, a direção de selo/emblema ganha muito mais força — vale decidir
antes de fechar a logo.
