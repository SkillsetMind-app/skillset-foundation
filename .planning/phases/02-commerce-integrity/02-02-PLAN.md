# Phase 2 Plan 02 - Subscription financial facts

## Objective

Make every recurring invoice visible and auditable in the same order, payment,
refund, and payout surfaces used by one-time purchases.

## Work

1. Materialize an immutable order and payment from every paid course-subscription invoice.
2. Write order, payment, and payout ledger in referentially valid order.
3. Accept subscription enrollments in the self-service refund policy.
4. Select the latest paid renewal deterministically for a refund.
5. Cancel recurring billing after a full invoice refund without revoking the enrollment as a one-time purchase.
6. Preserve partial-refund access and payout reversal behavior.
7. Cover mapping, refund policy, and idempotent Stripe cancellation with tests.

## Out of scope

- Historical invoice backfill from Stripe.
- Live production schema export while Supabase management credentials are unavailable.
- Creator subscriber center and recurring-revenue analytics.
